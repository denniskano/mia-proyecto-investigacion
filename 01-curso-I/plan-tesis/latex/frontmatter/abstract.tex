Before an integration we want to know whether a banking API's YAML describes the same business as
the BIAN Service Domain it is paired with. Today that check is done by eye, or it is not done.

In review we open the file and the service domain. We look at paths, each operationId and the
schema \$ref entries. A contract that says \texttt{paymentRequest} against a domain that says
\texttt{PaymentOrder}, or a behavior with no operation in the YAML, leaves no number on the record.
For that pair we compute meaning similarity, REST hierarchy match and coverage of BIAN concepts:
SemanticScore (S), StructuralScore (E) and CoverageScore (C). The three feed one AlignmentScore.
Appendix~C records the order.

This thesis includes the prototype that computes the score and a bounded pilot: seven Open
Finance Brasil instances against BIAN v14 service domains. The parser and the matching are in
scope. An issuing bank's YAML, scoring all 38 APIs against all 258 domains, and latency, fuzzing
or production security stay out (§\ref{sec:alcances}).
