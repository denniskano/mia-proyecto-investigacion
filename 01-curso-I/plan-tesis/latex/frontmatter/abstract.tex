OpenAPI files go live. BIAN Service Domains stay in architecture decks. We have not seen a
published score that puts the two artefacts side by side before release.

Group~10 writes that score. Meaning (S), REST shape versus BIAN behaviours (E), and how much of
the Service Domain the YAML covers (C) feed AlignmentScore. The consistency matrix is Appendix~C,
not a slogan in the introduction.

Guide~1 is the design. Parser, bank pilot and runtime security are later work
(§\ref{sec:alcances}).
